The Villa

Renovated in 2024, made for slow summers

Villa Eugenia is a two-storey beachside home inside the exclusive gated community of La Perla Marina, a few minutes' walk from a stunning, secluded stretch of coast. Renovated top to bottom in 2024, everything inside is brand new — from the furnishings to the modern kitchen.

Four spacious bedrooms sleep up to eight guests: three with king-size beds and a versatile fourth room set up as two twins or a king. A dedicated study with fast Wi-Fi (200+ Mbps) makes it easy to mix work and holiday.

Outside, the garden opens onto shaded dining under the pergola, a barbecue, and a lawn framed by Mediterranean pines. Your beach kit is ready to go: four sunbeds, a parasol and beach towels are all provided.
